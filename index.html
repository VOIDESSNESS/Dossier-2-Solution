Solar Infrastructure

SOLAR — PRIMARY GENERATION

Nigeria sits in the Sun Belt. The northern region receives 5.5–6.8 kWh/m²/day of solar irradiance — among the highest in the world, comparable to the Sahara. This is AMARA's primary strategic advantage. No fuel imports. No price volatility. No emissions. Structurally, it is the most defensible long-term energy investment Nigeria can make.

NATIONAL GRID TOPOLOGY

AMARA Grid is designed as a multi-node, intelligent mesh network — not a hub-and-spoke system. Every node communicates bidirectionally. Every transmission path has a redundant alternative. A failure at one node does not collapse the system. This is the fundamental architectural departure from the existing TCN structure that has collapsed over 100 times.

Smart Metering

AMARA SMART TERMINAL

Nigeria's 50 million electricity consumers will be served by a new generation of IoT-enabled smart meters — the AMARA Smart Terminal. These are not upgrades of the existing prepaid infrastructure. They are a complete technological replacement: two-way communicating, tamper-detecting, AI-integrated devices that provide both the company and the consumer with complete, real-time transparency.

📡
AMI Communication
Advanced Metering Infrastructure — 4G/5G + RF Mesh. Two-way data flow between terminal and NEXUS. Real-time consumption push every 15 minutes.
🔒
Tamper Detection
Magnetic field sensors, optical tamper detection, and current signature analysis detect bypass connections instantly. Automated alert to NEXUS and field teams.
⚡
Remote Connect/Disconnect
Full remote control of supply. Customer can toggle power from the AMARA Connect app. Operators can disconnect non-paying accounts instantly — no field visit required.
📊
Granular Consumption Data
Appliance-level load profiling via smart circuit integration. 15-minute interval data. Historical consumption, peak usage analysis, and anomaly detection.
🏠
Smart Circuit Integration
Links to AMARA Smart Circuit Box in the consumer's premises. Individual socket/circuit monitoring. Fault detection within the home network reported to the app.
🔐
Blockchain Audit Trail
Every transaction — top-up, consumption record, tariff change — is immutably recorded on AMARA's private blockchain. Fraud-proof billing. Consumer-verifiable records.
🔋
Battery Backup
Internal 72-hour backup battery ensures metering continues during outages. Consumption records preserved. Automatic sync on reconnection.
🌐
DLMS/COSEM Compliant
International Device Language Message Specification standard. Interoperable, standardised data format. Future-proof architecture compatible with ECOWAS smart grid evolution.

The Intelligence Layer

AMARA NEXUS — THE BRAIN

AMARA NEXUS is the AI operating system that governs the entire grid. It is not a monitoring dashboard — it is an autonomous decision-making system that continuously optimises power distribution, detects faults before they cascade, prevents fraud in real time, and communicates the state of the grid to every stakeholder level simultaneously. Humans are in the loop for escalation. NEXUS is in the loop for everything.
